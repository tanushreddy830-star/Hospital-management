## Features

### 👨‍⚕️ Doctor & Staff Management
* Manage doctor profiles, specialties, schedules, and availability.
* Assign staff roles and control access permissions.

### 🩺 Patient Management
* Patient registration, profile creation, and medical history tracking.
* Electronic Health Record (EHR) integration for quick diagnostic lookup.

### 📅 Appointment Scheduling
* Online and offline appointment booking.
* Real-time slot availability, cancellations, and status tracking.

### 💳 Billing & Financials
* Automated invoice generation for consultations, procedures, and pharmacy.
* Support for insurance claim status tracking and payment processing.

### 📦 Pharmacy & Inventory Control
* Real-time tracking of medicine stock, equipment, and medical consumables.
* Automatic alerts for low stock and expiring items.
